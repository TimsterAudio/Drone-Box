How this works

Thanks for testing! Work through the checks below with Drone Box open. Mark each one Pass, Fail or Skip (skip anything you can't try). When something fails, a box opens: say what happened instead.

Your answers are saved in this browser as you go, so you can stop and come back. When you're done, click Copy report or Download report and send the result back.

Use headphones or speakers at a low volume to start with. Drones can build up, and some settings get loud.
